Before you buy

Good to know.

Which products can I buy today?

The Ze sticker set is available through LINE, and registered members can choose any 6 of the 53 gallery wallpapers free. City Sessions Vol. 01 is a 12-image collection planned at US$4.99; its checkout is not open yet. Other paid collections remain in development.

Where will downloadable products be sold?

Free wallpapers are available in your member account. The first complete collection is being prepared for PayPal checkout, with DONGZE CULTURE & CREATIVITY CO., LTD. as the seller. Sales will open after payment and automatic-download tests pass. No payment is collected for City Sessions yet.

Does a sticker purchase include art files?

LINE sticker purchases are for use inside LINE. Downloadable creator assets will be separate products with their own file specifications and usage terms.

Can I use the artwork in my own products?

The phone kit is intended for personal use. Future creator packs will state their permitted uses on each product page. For character licensing, contact DONGZE before using our characters in merchandise or branding.

How do I get in touch?

Email dongze.ltd@gmail.com with the product name and your question.
