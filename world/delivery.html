Preview status

A clear path from artwork to your collection.

1 / Explore

Browse original preview artwork and read the product status. Drafts and planned formats are labeled separately.

2 / Confirm

Before any release, the exact files, price, use rights, seller terms and supported payment method must be confirmed.

3 / Receive

After launch, delivery must check the purchaser, confirmed payment, product version and any required license confirmation. These services are not connected in this preview.

What can I get from this preview?

You can view artwork previews and collection information. Checkout, member sign-in and original downloads are disabled. The Your downloads page explains the future access flow; it is not a connected customer library. There is no email signup or personal-data form here.

What about the official wallpaper member choices?

The official gallery describes six free choices after sign-in. That separate service is not connected here. Browsing this preview does not use a choice, sign you in or unlock a download. New redraws are not represented as part of that offering.

May I reuse or redistribute the artwork?

Viewing does not grant a new license. Each release needs its own confirmed terms. Do not assume permission for resale, public redistribution, commercial printing or merchandise.

What if I need help?

For an existing official order, use the support route in your order records or contact the PBC support team. No purchase is processed by this preview, and no new refund or delivery promise is made here.
