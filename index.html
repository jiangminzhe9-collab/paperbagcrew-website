02 / Our story

Big stories.
Small paper bags.

Paperbag Crew is an original character world by DONGZE Culture & Creativity, a creative studio in Taiwan.

We find inspiration in familiar places: the office coffee break, a late-night shop, a neighbourhood workshop. A simple paper bag becomes a starting point for humour, warmth and a character you can recognise.

From character art to comics, art toy concepts and digital stories, we’re building a world people can connect with, collect and share.
