LEAD STORY / COS-001

Lumen's First Dinner Agreement

Lumen follows a rhythmic signal to Paperbag Town. Her translator renders an invitation to dinner as a proposal for a lasting alliance, so she arrives at Ladle's kitchen with full diplomatic ceremony. Orbit, Lyra and Halo follow with trading, research and travel plans, until the residents feel overwhelmed. Lumen admits she has not understood what people actually want. She pauses the ceremony and asks what each household wishes to share or keep private. The final agreement is modest: bring a dish if you want to, let everyone speak and respect a refusal. The mysterious signal turns out to be Tempo's recording of dinner sounds. It still brings different worlds to the same table.

Character connections

Communication partner: COS-006 Lyra. Commercial friction: COS-002 Orbit. Local hosts: HER-009 Ladle and LIF-007 Tempo.

Character arc

From treating procedure as understanding, through recognising that good intentions can overwhelm people, to listening and asking again.

First-episode idea

Another Bowl, Please: Lumen mistakes a second helping for a treaty renewal and produces fresh documents. Ladle fills the bowl and asks her to pass the napkins.

COS-001 / LEAD

Lumen

DZ100-COS-001 · Original board no. 071

“Listen first. Then shake hands.”
Role
Alien envoy; collection lead
Visual identity
Opalescent diplomatic robes, spherical antennae and translucent staff.
Personality & blind spot
Curious and sincere; unfamiliar with indirect speech.
Current goal
Create a fair first exchange between two worlds.
Likes & habits
Collects greetings; an antenna glows while thinking.
Background
Investigating a space signal, Lumen mistakes an invitation to dinner for a major diplomatic agreement.
Story potential
Learns to understand kindness beyond translation, while introducing residents to ordinary alien life.
