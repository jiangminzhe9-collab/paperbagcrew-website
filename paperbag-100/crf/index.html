LEAD STORY / CRF-001

Timber and the Wish Workshop

Timber returns to town with a chipped wooden ruler, hoping to reopen his grandfather's workshop before the old street is redeveloped. After a storm, he discovers unfinished requests on the backs of old orders: a chair for someone coming home late, a display stand for a friend, a clock case still waiting for repair. He tries to finish them alone and falls further behind. Wrench repairs his tools, Sprig offers a courtyard and Tick recognises the dates on the orders. Timber finally understands that his grandfather left more than a list of jobs: he left a reason for neighbours to meet again. The workshop's first completed project is a table big enough for everyone.

Character connections

Workshop partner: CRF-002 Wrench. Creative contrast: CRF-006 Kiln. Keeper of clues: CRF-010 Tick.

Character arc

From equating reliability with doing everything alone, through a damaging mistake, to asking for help and becoming a connector in the neighbourhood.

First-episode idea

One More Chair: a customer repeatedly orders an extra chair. Timber assumes a business is expanding, then learns the customer is making room for an old friend. After finishing, Timber discovers he has spent the whole meal standing.

CRF-001 / LEAD

Timber

DZ100-CRF-001 · Original board no. 001

“A little crooked? We can line it up again.”
Role
Carpenter; collection lead
Visual identity
Indigo work apron, carpenter's square and hand saw.
Personality & blind spot
Steady and dependable, but reluctant to delegate.
Current goal
Reopen his grandfather's neighbourhood workshop.
Likes & habits
Loves reclaimed timber; taps the workbench three times before starting.
Background
After a storm damages the workshop, Timber finds unfinished wishes written on the backs of old orders.
Story potential
Repairs objects to reconnect people, then realises he has made everyone a chair except himself.
