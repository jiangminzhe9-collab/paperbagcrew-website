LEAD STORY / HER-002

Lifeline and the Eleventh Knot

Lifeline's rescue rope carries ten little knots, each marking a memorable journey home with a team. He believes a leader should climb first, leave last and never let a newcomer take a critical role. Then a sudden storm strands him during a search. He must listen to teammates below and trust a new recruit with his return. Afterwards, he spends time in town teaching residents to prepare equipment, recognise routes and report their location. The next rescue succeeds because more people know what to do. Lifeline hands responsibility to others again. The eleventh knot is for the teammates who brought him home.

Character connections

Rescue teammates: HER-001 Hose and HER-008 Brace. Community meeting place: HER-009 Ladle's kitchen.

Character arc

From believing a hero must never need rescue, through losing control, to trusting teammates and preparing others to take the lead.

First-episode idea

Only Buying Groceries: Lifeline gives a shopping trip routes, equipment and a meeting time. The team eventually has to find him lost among the spices.

HER-001

Hose

DZ100-HER-001 · Original board no. 051

“Get everyone outside first.”
Role
Firefighter
Visual identity
Yellow turnout gear, reflective stripes and a hose nozzle.
Personality & blind spot
Decisive and considerate; struggles to switch off while on leave.
Current goal
Help old-street residents build better fire-safety habits.
Likes & habits
Loves cooking for groups; notices the exits on arrival.
Background
Growing up in the old streets gives Hose a strong sense of how prevention and local knowledge support rescue.
Story potential
Invited to dinner, Hose first plans the restaurant's evacuation route.

HER-002 / LEAD

Lifeline

DZ100-HER-002 · Original board no. 052

“Up together. Home together.”
Role
Mountain rescuer; collection lead
Visual identity
Red mountain-rescue suit, rope pack and climbing clips.
Personality & blind spot
Brave and attentive; too hard on himself after mistakes.
Current goal
Build a rescue team in which everyone can take responsibility.
Likes & habits
Loves rope knots; writes down a return time before entering the mountains.
Background
Getting stranded during a solo rescue teaches Lifeline that rescuers need to accept help too.
Story potential
Brings expedition-level planning to a shopping trip, then needs the team to find him in the spice aisle.
