LEAD STORY / WAR-001

Bastion's Way Home

A border storm cuts off an outpost from the supply city. Bastion must lead a makeshift squad through the storm with an energy core. He hides the fact that, on this same route, waiting for the perfect order once cost a squad its chance to withdraw. Determined to absorb every risk, he pushes his shield beyond its limits. Rivet's mech needs repairs, Winglet's flight gear fails and Patch warns that nobody can keep going indefinitely. At the storm's centre, Bastion finally shares the truth and lets each teammate take responsibility for part of the escape. They deliver the core and return together. The new dents in his shield belong to a shared journey, not a solitary victory.

Character connections

Engineering partner: WAR-003 Rivet. The person who challenges his blind spot: WAR-010 Patch. Route support: WAR-002 Dune.

Character arc

From overprotecting others to avoid repeating the past, through the failure of his shield, to sharing information and trusting the squad.

First-episode idea

Behind the Shield: Bastion thinks the squad is waiting for him to lead. Behind him, they are fixing his pack and warming his lunch.

Bastion

DZ100-WAR-001 · Original board no. 021

“Behind me. Then forward, together.”
Role
Shield infantry; collection lead
Visual identity
Heavy steel shield, dark armour and a grounded stance.
Personality & blind spot
Reliable and stubborn; tries to absorb every risk alone.
Current goal
Bring the whole squad safely through the border storm belt.
Likes & habits
Enjoys repairing his shield; checks his teammates' straps before departure.
Background
Once, waiting for a perfect order cost the squad its chance to withdraw. Bastion will not let it happen again.
Story potential
Learns that accepting cover from teammates makes his shield stronger, not less useful.
