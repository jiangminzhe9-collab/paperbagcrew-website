LEAD STORY / VIL-001

Velvet and the Ownerless Collection

Whenever an object with a doubtful history reaches auction, Velvet sends an elegant calling card. He claims to return things to their rightful place, while following clues to his family's scattered collection. One job leads to a child who treasures a music box as a memory of a mother. For once, Velvet cannot justify taking it. Cipher then uncovers uncomfortable evidence about the family's own dealings. As Ivory prepares to destroy the records, Velvet must choose between protecting the family name and revealing the whole history. He publishes the ledger and helps owners reclaim their objects. His taste for theatrical entrances survives: the next job still begins with a beautiful card.

Character connections

Opponent in trade: VIL-002 Ivory. Information contact: VIL-004 Cipher. A darker reflection of collecting: VIL-006 Gilt.

Character arc

From dressing possession up as fairness, through acknowledging family responsibility, to exposing the truth instead of deciding ownership for everyone else.

First-episode idea

The Calling Card: Velvet's carefully worded warning is mistaken for a wedding invitation. The recipient asks whether dinner is included, and the job stalls over printing and wording.

VIL-001 / LEAD

Velvet

DZ100-VIL-001 · Original board no. 041

“Before I take it, who does it belong to?”
Role
Gentleman thief; collection lead
Visual identity
Burgundy suit, dark cape, lock tools and white cravat.
Personality & blind spot
Elegant and clever; assumes his idea of fairness is everyone's.
Current goal
Discover how his family's collection was taken.
Likes & habits
Collects old invitation cards; straightens his cuffs before a job.
Background
Velvet calls reclaiming objects justice until he meets new owners who cherish them.
Story potential
His story challenges possessiveness and shifts from taking objects to exposing their histories.

“Everything has a price.”
Role
Underworld broker
Visual identity
Ivory pinstriped suit, ornate shoulder cape and cane.
Personality & blind spot
Smooth and patient; cannot tolerate losing control.
Current goal
Control the town's most important deals.

Ivory rises from cargo handler to broker and begins treating every relationship as a debt.

Can calculate interest perfectly but cannot make sense of a free favour.
