# Sports Crew portrait asset audit

Audited 2026-10-30. Eleven existing characters and both team artworks are retained. This is production preparation, not eleven finished trading cards.

Eight characters have earlier individual artwork candidates (nine files including two coach versions); three have no located single original. Zero final portraits were verified against the current design and individual approval. No candidate has been substituted into the live roster.

The earlier coach images have a chin beard or no mustache. The earlier #23 image has light skin and conflicts with Ace's approved Black identity. The old #11 player does not match current Dash. Other baseball singles differ in branding, cap or proportions. These are reference candidates only.

See [portrait-manifest.json](portrait-manifest.json) for each existing character's code, name, number, role, personality, quote, source filename, discrepancy, visual specification and proposed pose. `portrait_image: null` and `card_status: incomplete` are intentional. A team image must never be counted as a single portrait.

Scope: current main repository trees, roster pages, Dongze catalogue, targeted Library title/content searches and visual inspection of candidate PNGs and both website WebPs. This is not an assertion that no originals exist elsewhere. The candidate PNGs have alpha channels; edge quality and final artwork approval remain unverified.

Production: create each full-body portrait separately; no group-image crops. Transparent PNG masters, no card frame or dialogue. Preserve chibi paper-bag styling, number/identity, original DZ/PAPERBAG marks and position-specific height/build. Verify each image before connecting it. No crowns or third-party marks. Proposed canvas and poses in the manifest are production proposals, not previously approved deliverables.
