BB / BASEBALL

Baseball Crew

Left to right: A-Zhun · Da-Pao · A-Bu · Lao-Bao · Tie-Mian · Xiao-Qing · Tap the artwork to enlarge.

BB-001#01

A-Zhun

Pitcher

A perfectionist who trusts his control, then overthinks the next pitch. A-Bu is the one teammate who calls him out.

“That was the wind.”
BB-002#99

Da-Pao

Power hitter

A stocky power hitter with unwavering confidence. Even after a swing and a miss, he believes the next one is leaving the park.

“Timing was a little off.”
BB-003#02

A-Bu

Catcher

The calmest voice on the field. He handles pitches and personalities, especially when A-Zhun keeps shaking off his signs.

“Can you read my signs?”
BB-004#88

Lao-Bao

Coach

The veteran coach with a distinctive gray mustache. He looks composed, but worries more than anyone when the plan falls apart.

“Hold on. Let me think.”
BB-005OFFICIAL

Tie-Mian

Umpire

An independent umpire with a straight face and very few words. He stays neutral through every protest.

“Call stands.”
BB-006#07

Xiao-Qing

Cheerleader

An upbeat cheerleader who finds something worth cheering for even after a loss. Sometimes her energy outpaces the entire team.

“Come on, make some noise!”

BK / BASKETBALL

Basketball Starting Five

Left to right: Dash · Ace · Chill · Brick · Tower · Tap the artwork to enlarge.

BK-001#11

Dash

Point guard · PG

The shortest player and the quickest thinker. He directs every possession, sometimes sending a clever pass before his teammates catch on.

“That was a pass. You were supposed to move.”
BK-002#23

Ace

Shooting guard · SG

A confident Black scoring guard who wants the ball when it matters. After a miss, he quietly puts in extra practice—and denies it.

“You guarding me or watching?”
BK-003#07

Chill

Small forward · SF

Versatile and unflappable. He covers for his teammates, says little, and lands every dry remark.

“Nice speech. Try defense.”
BK-004#34

Brick

Power forward · PF

Strong, loyal, and dependable on screens and rebounds. He is convinced he has a three-point shot. His teammates remain unconvinced.

“I call that a rebound opportunity.”
BK-005#55

Tower

Center · C

The tallest teammate: a gentle Black center with a formidable presence. He wishes everyone knew that rebounding takes more than just being tall.

“The paint is closed.”

Banter belongs to the moment. Their stories grow through practice, mistakes, teamwork, and friendship.
